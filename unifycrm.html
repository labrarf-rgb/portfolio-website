UnifyCRM

Prototype Nonprofit CRM Web App

UnifyCRM is a prototype constituent relationship management system designed specifically for nonprofit organizations. Most CRM tools are built for sales pipelines — UnifyCRM is built around the relationships and workflows that actually matter to nonprofits: who your constituents are, how they've contributed, and how they show up to serve your mission.

What it does

  • Constituent management. Maintain a central record of donors, volunteers, and supporters — contact information, engagement history, and relationship notes in one place.
  • Donation tracking. Log and view donation history by constituent, track giving trends over time, and keep a clear record of what your community has contributed.
  • Volunteer management. Track volunteer hours, assignments, and participation history so coordinators always know who's active and what they've done.
  • Reporting. Surface insights across constituents, donations, and volunteer activity — built for program staff who need answers without needing a data team.
  • Volunteer kiosk. A self-service check-in and check-out interface that lets volunteers log their own time on-site, reducing coordinator overhead and improving data accuracy.
