Find the poetic form you did not know you were looking for.

There are hundreds of poetic forms and almost no way to find the one that suits you, short of reading your way through all of them. This quiz asks twenty four short questions about what you want from a line, then matches you against twenty four forms, from the sonnet to the Burmese thanbauk.

You get one form, three poems in it worth your time, the rules in case you ever want to try writing one, and a shelf of four more that came close.

It takes about three minutes. Nothing is saved and nothing leaves your browser.

      Most of what this model knows about a form is factual, but how funny or how private a form is are judgment calls. Answering here records your trait scores and this result so those calls can be fitted to real responses later. It is saved in this browser only, and nothing is sent anywhere.

      A note on honesty: this is entertainment with a real model underneath, not a validated instrument. Most of each form's profile is description rather than opinion, since a villanelle does repeat and a haiku is short, but how witty or how private a form is are judgment calls, and the catalog is twenty four forms out of hundreds. Treat the result as a good place to start looking, not a verdict.
